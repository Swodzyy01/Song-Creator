# v1.1.0

- The level now starts on the floor: everything is lowered by a configurable number of blocks (default 2, setting "Lower the level").
- New **Start at** slider: choose where in the song the level begins (the level plays the song from there).
- Much more varied cube gameplay: 21 patterns (block + spike, spike to platform, stairs, platform + spike, pad to platform, towers ...), every part of the song gets its own flavour (spike rhythm, block runs, pads and air, tight timing), patterns follow the kind of hit (kick, snare, hat) and never repeat three times in a row.
- Ship, UFO and wave are no longer plain block tunnels: 45 degree slopes, spikes on the walls, rows of teeth, floating obstacles, pinch gates, zigzags and stairs. The corridors follow the melody of the song.
- Speeds are mixed: even Extreme Demon uses 1x and 2x in calm parts and 3x/4x on the drops. Never the same vehicle twice in a row.
- Frame perfect is no longer a whole level of it: a few tight moments per section, never narrower than 3 frames in the simulation, always followed by easy patterns. Wave corridors are at least 3 rows wide.
- Decoration: colours drift every bar, drops get a flash and a screen shake, vines / lights / saws / pointed tips above the floor, zigzag ceilings on drops, corridor walls with bright edges.
- New settings: Lower the level, Use slopes, Mirror slopes.
